Paste the waypoint: lines into your Xaero waypoints file for the End (the XaeroWaypoints folder, then the dim%1 subfolder inside your world), or import them any other way. The color from green to purple shows the order.
How it works

Search. Uses the same world generation rules as the game: the 20×20-chunk region grid, a biome check on the outer islands, a terrain check (height of at least 60 under all four corners of the city) and assembly of the towers and bridges to see whether the city has a ship. The algorithm is ported from cubiomes; the output was compared with a C program on several seeds and versions, and every coordinate matched.

Route. Greedy insertion, then reordering (2-opt, Or-opt), swapping ships for better-placed ones, and random restarts. Distances are straight lines in the X/Z plane; height is ignored.

Limits. Java Edition only, 1.13 and newer. Bedrock generates the world differently, so its seeds don't match. Y in the table is the level of the ship's hull bottom.
